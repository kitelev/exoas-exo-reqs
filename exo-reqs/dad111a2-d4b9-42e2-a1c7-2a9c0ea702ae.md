## Job story

When a spec's `beforeAll` overflows its 60 s budget while `ObsidianLauncher.launch()` is still inside a launch attempt (plugin wait, vault wait, window wait) and Playwright runs `afterAll` → `launcher.close()`, I want `launch()` to stop right there — no backoff, no `launchAttempt(N+1)` — so that no second Obsidian is spawned on CDP :9222 for a caller that has already left, and the next spec's own `beforeAll` finds the port free.

## Context

- Source: ticket `81977587-7c8a-4fa9-8e9c-ee58edea6b8a` (LOW-3 of the ORCH review of PR kitelev/exocortex#4249; parent techbacklog `bbac67ce`). Sibling of req `675d37a6-054a-4acb-873b-cba9e90c6d3e`, which governs *what* `waitForPluginReady` reports when `close()` nulls the window concurrently (an explicit "window closed during plugin wait … abandoned by its caller" error). What `launch()` then does with that error is outside `675d37a6` — this requirement closes that half. `675d37a6` is not amended.
- Mechanism (`packages/obsidian-plugin/tests/e2e/utils/obsidian-launcher.ts` @ `e3d79024`): `launch()` (`:26-61`) runs `launchAttempt` up to 3 times and, on ANY error, calls `close()` for cleanup, sleeps `2 s × attempt` and retries. The explicit abandon error from `waitForPluginReady` (`:404-406`) takes exactly that path. The same happens for the un-named forms of the same event: after `close()` the window-wait loop (`:178-192`) and `waitForVaultReady` (`:453`) fail with `TypeError … (reading 'evaluate')` and are retried too. Reproduced with a unit fixture on `e3d79024`: `launchAttempt` mocked to call `launcher.close()` and throw the explicit error → `launchAttempt` was called **2** times and `launch()` resolved (the second, unattended spawn "succeeded").
- Design (plan-gate, ORCH `main-35699` 2026-09-17 PLAN-OK, option a): `launch()` marks itself in-flight for its whole duration and clears a per-launch `abandoned` flag at start; the public `close()` sets `abandoned` when a launch is in flight and then runs the teardown; `launch()`'s own per-attempt cleanup goes through the private `teardown()` (never through `close()`), and `launch()` checks `abandoned` after the cleanup AND after the backoff sleep, throwing an explicit "abandoned by its caller — not retrying" error instead of spawning again. Abandonment is a property of the launcher lifecycle (who called `close()`), not of the error text — so every failure form after a caller's `close()` is covered, and `waitForPluginReady` / the trust-dialog handling are not touched.

## Statement (Gherkin)

```gherkin
Feature: ObsidianLauncher.launch() does not retry a launch its caller has abandoned

  Background:
    Given ObsidianLauncher.launch() retries launchAttempt up to 3 times with a 2 s × attempt backoff on failure
    And a spec's afterAll may call launcher.close() while launch() is still inside an attempt

  Scenario: close() during a launch attempt ends launch() without a second attempt
    Given launchAttempt 1 is running and the caller invokes close() before the attempt fails
    When the attempt then fails with the explicit "window closed during plugin wait" error
    Then launch() rejects with an error naming the abandonment ("abandoned by its caller") and that it is not retrying
    And launchAttempt is called exactly once
    And no backoff sleep happens before the rejection

  Scenario: close() during a launch attempt covers the un-named failure forms too (control for breadth)
    Given launchAttempt 1 is running and the caller invokes close() before the attempt fails
    When the attempt then fails with a TypeError (the window-wait / vault-wait form: "Cannot read properties of null")
    Then launch() rejects with the abandonment error and launchAttempt is called exactly once

  Scenario: close() during the backoff pause ends launch() before the next attempt
    Given launchAttempt 1 failed with an ordinary error and launch() is sleeping its backoff
    When the caller invokes close() during that pause
    Then launch() rejects with the abandonment error after the pause
    And launchAttempt is not called a second time

  Scenario: an ordinary failure is still retried (pair, behaviour unchanged)
    Given launchAttempt 1 fails with an ordinary error and nobody calls close()
    When the 2 s backoff elapses
    Then launchAttempt 2 runs and, if it succeeds, launch() resolves

  Scenario: a new launch() on the same instance after an abandoned one starts clean
    Given a previous launch() on this launcher was abandoned
    When launch() is called again and attempt 1 fails with an ordinary error
    Then the backoff runs and attempt 2 is tried (the abandoned flag does not leak into the next launch)

  Scenario: close() after a completed launch behaves as before (control)
    Given launch() has resolved
    When the caller invokes close()
    Then close() runs the normal teardown and does NOT log that it is abandoning an in-flight launch
```

## Non-goals

- Not changing `waitForPluginReady`, the trust-dialog re-probe (`clickTrustDialogIfVisible` / `handleTrustDialog`), the specs, the shard assignments, `playwright-e2e.config.ts` or CI workflows.
- Not changing the retry count (3) or the backoff schedule for ordinary failures.
- Not making `close()` and `launch()`'s own cleanup mutually exclusive when they overlap (both go through the same teardown; the overlap existed before and is tolerated by the teardown's null checks).
