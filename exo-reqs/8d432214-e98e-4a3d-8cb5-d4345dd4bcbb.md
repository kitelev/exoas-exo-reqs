## Job Story

When the per-repo CI gate (`exoas-ci` → `assetspace-ci.yml` step 6, `audit assetspace-depends --self <owner/repo>`) runs over a merged vault that is **incomplete** — a declared `exo__AssetSpace_dependsOn` target could not be cloned (private repo, no usable token; 15 of 62 registered AssetSpaces depend on a private one, 2026-09-13) — I want the verb to **degrade explicitly** instead of counting every reference into the absent dependency as *uncovered*, so that the gate keeps failing for references into **undeclared** AssetSpaces while a declared-but-absent dependency is reported loudly (its own counter, its own line, `OK (DEGRADED …)`, exit 0) — and never silently as either PASS or FAIL. Source: ticket `fe1d3ef3` (N2 tail of program `31c2bdee`); the workflow already promised «refs into it become warnings», the verb did not honour it (`exoas-shared-private` runs 34768706122 / 34768708844: `uncovered by CLOSURE: 18`, all 18 unresolved → `$shared-identities`).
